This report outlines the strategy for a **Product Launch** campaign targeting **High Spenders** with an active budget of **$90,000** over **6 months**, aiming for **maximum engagement** and high long-term ROI. The strategy focuses on affluent individuals and businesses, leveraging **LinkedIn Ads (40%)**, **Google Search Ads (PPC) (35%)**, and **Tier 1 PR & Industry Media Outreach (25%)** for precise targeting and credibility.

Key estimates include a Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of ***$500 - $2000*** per customer and an expectation of **high long-term ROI**. Significant risks, such as inaccurate target audience definition and underperforming channels due to the absence of prior market research, are addressed with proactive mitigation strategies including rapid qualitative research, aggressive A/B testing, and agile budget reallocation. The campaign emphasizes lead generation, conversion optimization, and brand authority to drive initial sales and foster high customer lifetime value.
